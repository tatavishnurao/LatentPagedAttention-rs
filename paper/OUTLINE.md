## Contradictions and gaps found in the repo (for the stop report)

1. **T2's ~20 ms is not in any committed file** (GAP above).
2. **"80 %" of dry-run E4 layers at 210 MHz** (in `docs/LOCAL_4060_RESULTS.md` and two commit messages) is wrong: the file gives 73 % exactly at 210 MHz and an in-band fraction of 0.09. The paper uses 73 % / 0.09 and the ledger records the correction.
3. **"+50 % score MMAs"** in the brief: score MMAs double; total per step is +50 %.
4. **"HMMA only with >= 16 rows"**: rows between 4 and 16 were never tested.
5. **"~16x slower"** is from one VOID process at one configuration (E4 B1_N1024); the C1-harness idle-clock factor is ~7.5x. Use "up to ~16x in one process".
6. **"Slow output writes" (T1) belong to the A1/C1 harness of 2026-10-02**, not to E3/E4; the paper should not imply E3/E4 timing had that defect.
7. `docs/01_literature_review.md` names works but gives **no references**; every citation must be verified at its source or left as `[TODO cite]`.
8. No ACM template (`acmart.cls`) or pandoc is installed; `pdflatex` and `latexmk` are. Default: Markdown with pandoc-ready structure, page count estimated from word count.
